The last contact [Roblox Developer gamejam] (Feb 06 2026) - (Feb 09 2026)



Developement History

I REALLY REALLY LOVE THIS PROYECT (before OUTSTEP)

This game was the first time i participate on a gamejam (spoiler i didnt won), we have like 72 hours limit to make a game with the temathic FIRST contact (heh get it the last contact heheh)

but there is a problem, i didnt haved enough time to make the game in 72 hours so i made it in 24 hours, i really liked the resuts due to the short time i had


I have plans to continue the game ;3c

Gameplay

The main gameplay was simple, you're a person who get separated to someone important to you after a Last Contact, then you get teleported to a strange "pacific" world

I love the camera mechanics that provides a 2.5D perspective also inspired from Crash Bandicoot also the cam changes in certain parts

The character mechanics are also simple: Wall Jump, M1's and also you needed to defeat some enemies

The game basicly was just a infinite linear run defeating enemies and like every 30 rooms you get into a space place where you can get some boosts (i didnt finished this)
